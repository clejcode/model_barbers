Who we are

The shop

A neighborhood shop.

Model Barbers is Williamsburg's neighborhood barbershop at 497 Union Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11211. Roman, Jack, and Alik do the cutting. Walk-ins only. Cash only.

It started as a family business and stayed that way — run by people who take pride in the work, not the brand. Regulars come back for the conversation and the cut. In a neighborhood of $70 haircuts, Model Barbers charges $30 and sends you out a brand new man. The list is on the services page.

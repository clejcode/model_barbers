The shop

A neighborhood shop that earned its reputation the old way.

Model Barbers has been on Union Ave for as long as most people in the neighborhood can remember. It started as a family business and it's stayed that way — run by people who take pride in the work, not the brand.

Roman, Jack, and Alik haven't built a customer base. They've built a clientele. There's a difference. Regular clients don't come back just for the cut. They come back for the conversation, the consistency, and the fact that you walk out looking better than when you walked in — every single time.

In a neighborhood where haircuts cost $70 and come with a mood board consultation, Model Barbers charges $30 and sends you out a brand new man. That's not a coincidence. It's the whole point.

"Worth the trek from LA."
Google Review — Roman's client, returning after moving across the country

The team

Roman, Jack & Alik

Roman

Roman will take his time with your cut — every single one. He's not cutting hair to get through the queue. He's building something that holds up in a week, in a month, in a year. His regulars have followed him through everything. Some fly in from LA just to sit in his chair. That's not loyalty you buy. That's loyalty you earn, one cut at a time.

Jack

You'll feel welcome the second you walk through the door. That's Jack. He's the kind of barber who remembers how you like it before you've said a word, who keeps the conversation going just enough, and who sends you out the door with a cut that does the talking for you. His regulars have been coming in for years, and they'll keep coming.

Alik

Fast doesn't mean rushed. Alik moves with precision — in and out in 20 minutes without cutting corners. Walk-ins who sit in Alik's chair once tend to ask for him by name the next time. He's consistent in a way that's harder to find than most people realize.

We're cash only. We don't have an app. We don't take appointments online. We open at 9. We'll see you when you get here.

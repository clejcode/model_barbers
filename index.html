Did Model Barbers move?

Yes. Model Barbers is now at 497 Union Ave, Brooklyn, NY 11211, just down the street from the old spot at 452 Union Ave.

02

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-ins are always welcome, and you do not need an appointment. Model Barbers does not take appointments online. If you're on a tight schedule, call (718) 387-9487 and we'll tell you the current wait.

03

How long does a haircut take?

Most cuts are done in 15–20 minutes. No unnecessary waiting around.

04

How much is a haircut?

A men's haircut is $30, a shape-up is $15, a beard trim is $15, and a straight razor shave is $30. Fades, head shaves, and hot towel shaves are on the services page. Cash only. Prices were confirmed as of 2025; call to verify.
